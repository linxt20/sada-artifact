### Important Caveats

1. **Selection Bias:** Top performers are skewed toward high-priority goals (84.5% Critical/High vs. 38.1% in full portfolio). Causality is bidirectional: better practices may enable priority, and priority may enable practice investment.

2. **Goal State Confound:** 48.4% of all goals reach "Completed" status; others remain "In Progress" (36%), "Planned" (11%), or "Cancelled" (4%). Top performers are from the completed subset, so this estimate assumes scope and priority remain constant.

3. **Measurement Paradox:** Low performers *mention* measurement (45.6%) almost as often as top performers (57.3%), suggesting *implementation quality* (not existence) of measurement systems is the differentiator—not captured in text analysis.

4. **Incomplete Attribution:** Descriptions reflect stated practices, not execution discipline. Management rigor, accountability, and organizational readiness are unobserved but likely material.
